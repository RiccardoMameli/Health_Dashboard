Four rules it enforces
  • A gap is drawn as a gap. The missing night is a dashed outline and the unavailable metrics are em-dashes, never a zero and never a smoothed line.
  • The phase is always on screen. The pill says baseline, so the brief states deviations and claims no causes.
  • Status colour is not the accent. Green, amber and red mean one thing each, and always carry a word beside them.
  • No number is a black box. Every figure opens its definition, its inputs and its source.
